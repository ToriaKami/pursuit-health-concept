About Jordan Lynch

I spent fifteen years inside benefits technology, and I watched the same thing happen over and over: the best sales pitch won, even when it was the wrong fit for the company buying it.

Nobody was lying. The person across the table believed in what they sold, because they only sold one thing. That is the whole problem. If you represent one model, every company that walks in needs that model.

So I left, and I built Pursuit to work the other way round. I price a group plan, a PEO, and a health budget against what you pay now, and I show you the math on all three. Sometimes the answer is that your current plan is fine and you should keep it. I would rather tell you that in the first meeting than sell you something you will resent in eighteen months.

I run Pursuit myself, from Centennial, Colorado. When you call, you get me.
