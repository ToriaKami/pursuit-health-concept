The explainer

What is an ICHRA?

An ICHRA, which stands for individual coverage health reimbursement arrangement, is a way for an employer to give employees a tax-free monthly amount to buy their own individual health insurance, instead of offering one group plan for everyone.

The employer sets the budget. The employee picks the plan.

Context image placeholder — an employee choosing their own plan

01

What does ICHRA stand for?

Individual Coverage Health Reimbursement Arrangement. Say it as “ick-rah.” The “individual coverage” part is the key: employees buy individual plans, the kind you would buy for yourself, rather than a single company plan.

02

How does an ICHRA work?

Three steps. The employer decides how much to offer each month. The employee buys an individual plan that fits them. The employer reimburses them, tax-free, up to the budget. The cost to the employer is fixed at the budget, which is the whole appeal.

03

How is an ICHRA different from a group plan?

A group plan is one plan the employer chooses for everyone, at a price the carrier sets and raises. An ICHRA is a budget the employer sets, and each employee chooses their own plan. Control of the plan moves to the employee. Control of the cost moves to the employer.

04

Who is an ICHRA a good fit for?

Employers whose renewal keeps climbing, who have a team spread across different places or life stages, or who want to offer coverage for the first time without committing to a group plan. It is a weaker fit for a small, similar team that is happy with a good group rate.

05

Is there a minimum or maximum budget?

No federal minimum or maximum. The employer decides. Amounts can differ by employee class, for example full-time versus part-time, or by family size, within the rules.

Common ICHRA questions

Is an ICHRA taxable to the employee?+

No. Reimbursements are tax-free to the employee when the plan is a qualifying individual health plan.

Can employees keep their own doctor?+

Yes. Because they choose their own plan, they can choose one their doctor accepts.

What is the difference between an ICHRA and a QSEHRA?+

Both are reimbursement arrangements. An ICHRA has no company-size limit and no cap on the amount. A QSEHRA is for smaller employers and is capped.

Does an ICHRA count as offering coverage under the ACA?+

Yes, when it meets the affordability rules, which is part of what we handle.
