For private equity

One benefits play across a portfolio of different companies

Health benefits are a rising, unpredictable cost line at every company you hold, and no two of them are the same size or shape. A health budget turns that line into a fixed number you set, and it works the same whether the portco has 12 employees or 200. One approach, priced per company.

Book a call

Why it works at the portfolio level

A group plan has to be negotiated company by company, and every renewal is a surprise.

A health budget is a number your operators control, set at each portco, benchmarked the same way across all of them. It makes benefits a lever you can actually pull, not a cost you brace for.

What it does for the operating thesis

Predictable

A fixed benefits cost at each portco, set annually, no mid-year surprises.

Portable

The same structure across the portfolio, so diligence and reporting look the same everywhere.

Handled

Enrollment, payroll integration, and ACA compliance are run at each company, not added to your operators’ workload.

Where it does not fit

Not every portco should switch. A company with a young, concentrated team and a good group rate may be better left alone.

I will tell you which of your companies are candidates and which are not, before anyone signs anything.
