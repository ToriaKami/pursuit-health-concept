The mechanics

How it works, start to finish

Most people assume a health budget must have a catch, because everything else in insurance does. It does not. You decide the number, your team spends it on their own plans, and the paperwork is ours. Here is every step, including the parts other people leave out.

01

We price your options, free

You send me what you pay now and how many people you cover. I come back with a group plan, a PEO, and a health budget priced side by side against your current setup. If your current plan wins, I tell you that, and we stop here.

02

You set the budget

If a health budget is the right call, you decide what you can afford per person each month. You can set different amounts for different groups, say more for a manager than a new hire, entirely within the rules. Your total cost is this number times your headcount. That is the whole formula.

03

Your team picks their plans, with help

Each employee chooses their own plan from the carriers they already know, with a licensed advisor walking them through it. They can pick the plan their own doctor takes. Nobody is left staring at a marketplace alone, and no question is too small.

04

We run it in the background

Payroll integration, the ACA reporting, the compliance. You approve the budget once a year and answer the occasional question. Everything else is handled.

How long it takes

A first review takes a week. A full setup, from yes to your team enrolling, usually runs three to five weeks depending on your renewal date. We work backward from your renewal so nothing lapses.

What it costs to run

Forty dollars per enrolled employee per month. No setup fee, no platform fee. The full breakdown is on the pricing page.
