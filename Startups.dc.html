“We cannot afford it yet.”

You set the budget. Fifty dollars a month per person is a real benefit and a known cost. You are not signing up for whatever a carrier decides next year.

“We will outgrow whatever we pick.”

You will not outgrow this. The budget scales with you, one person at a time, and it works the same at 8 people and 80.

“We do not have anyone to run it.”

You do not need anyone. The enrollment help, the payroll piece, the ACA reporting, all of it is ours.
