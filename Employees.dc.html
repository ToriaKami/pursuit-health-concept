Your company set a health budget. Here is what it means for you

If you just heard your health insurance is changing, your first thought was probably that this is a quiet way to give you less. It is a fair worry, and the honest answer is no. In most cases you end up with more choice than you had before, not less. Here is exactly what changes.

Your three worries, answered

Will I lose my doctor?

No, and this is the part people are most surprised by. You choose your own plan now, which means you can choose the one your doctor already takes. A group plan is the option that could not let you do that.

Will I pay more?

Your employer gives you a set amount each month to spend on a plan. If you pick a plan that costs less than your budget, you keep the difference in effect. If you want a richer plan, you can put your own money toward it. You are in control of the trade, not your HR department.

Am I on my own to figure this out?

No. A licensed advisor walks you through it, compares the plans for you, and answers your questions for as long as it takes. It costs you nothing, and there is no such thing as a question too basic.

Your money can go further than premiums

There is one more thing. Some of the money you already set aside for health, in an HSA or FSA, can go toward the things that keep you well in the first place, like a gym membership or supplements.
