Three ways to cover your team, priced side by side

Including who each one is wrong for, because that is the part nobody tells you.

01

A group plan

What most companies have

One plan, chosen by you, for everyone. The simplest thing to explain and the hardest thing to predict. You control the plan but not the price.

Right when your team is small, similar, and happy with what they have.

02

A PEO

Admin off your plate

You join a much larger pool, so you get rates a business your size could never negotiate alone. Payroll and compliance usually come with it.

Right when you want the paperwork gone, on someone else’s terms.

03

A health budget

Known cost

You decide what you can afford per person. Your employees each pick their own plan and their own doctor with it. Your cost is set before the year starts and it does not move.

Right when your rate keeps climbing, or your people are spread out.
